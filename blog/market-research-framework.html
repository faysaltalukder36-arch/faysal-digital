Market Research

A Simple Market Research Framework for Small Businesses

A repeatable starting point for reviewing competitors, audience needs, positioning and growth opportunities before increasing marketing spend.

Draft article prepared for editing. Replace or expand this content with your own verified examples, screenshots and experience before publishing.

Define the decision

Good research starts with a decision. Are you choosing a market, refining an offer, entering a new location or trying to improve customer acquisition?

Map the customer

Describe who the customer is, what problem they are solving, what alternatives they consider and what information helps them trust an offer.

Study competitors

Review competing offers, positioning, pricing signals, messaging, reviews and customer experience. Look for patterns and gaps—not just a list of competitor names.

Turn research into action

The output should be a set of decisions: who to target, what to say, what to test, and what evidence would change your mind.

What to do next

Turn the framework into a checklist for your next campaign or market decision. Keep the final article specific, evidence-based and useful to the reader.
